## Deliberately not built

- **Quantity discounts and an upsell before checkout.** They push the basket size, and the data I had suggests that costs first orders.
- **Real geolocation.** A permission prompt and a third-party service during a live demo; the button shows the idea with a sample address.

## Rules I'd give the agent up front next time

1. Business values only from config.
2. Never add to the cart without confirmation.
3. Check every change in the browser, on mobile too.
